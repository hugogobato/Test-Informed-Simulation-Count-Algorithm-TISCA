# P5.5-T2: J-justification classifications for Remaining_Papers/Folder_1

## Methods

This is a row-level audit of the downloaded Folder_1 set only. The stable identifier is the corpus row in `remaining_papers_manual_check.md`, not the paper ID. The set contains 21 unique readable PDFs and 22 corpus records because `Scotina2019` occurs in rows 68 and 95. The two local copies of `1809.00268v2.pdf` are byte-identical, so only `1809.00268v2.pdf` is recorded. Zone.Identifier files were ignored.

The local PDFs were the primary evidence. Titles, authors, DOI or arXiv identifiers, and paper content were used to match files to checklist rows. I read the simulation-methods sections, relevant tables, appendices, and locally bundled supplementary material where available. The primary corpus value `J_numeric` was coded, and other numerical repetitions were recorded separately. A count-specific precision, Monte Carlo-standard-error, power, pilot-variance, computational-budget, or other criterion is `explicit`. A recognizable convention or cited precedent without a formal calculation is `implicit_or_convention`, with criterion `precedent`. A reported count with no count-specific reason is `unjustified`, with criterion `none`. A non-unique corpus-to-paper mapping is `unclear_report`, with criterion `unclear`. `not_applicable` is reserved for a missing numeric J or unreadable full text.

Generic DGP, sample-size, estimator, prior, matching, confidence-level, bootstrap, cross-validation, posterior-draw, MCMC, and optimization explanations were not counted unless explicitly tied to the outer replication count. `justification_status` uses `justification_present`, `missing_justification`, and `unclear_reporting`. `J_is_outer_replication` uses `Y`, `N`, or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. `sensitivity_to_J` is `yes` only for a direct variation of J or a direct study of Monte Carlo error as a function of J.

The complete field-level record is in [J_justification_classifications_Folder_1.csv](/home/hugo_souto/Stuff/Submitted_Research/TISCA_paper/Remaining_Papers/J_justification_classifications_Folder_1.csv). The table below provides one auditable row per corpus record.
