# P5.5-T2: J-justification classifications for `Remaining_Papers/Folder_2`

## Methods

This is a row-level audit of the downloaded `Folder_2` set. The stable identifier is the corpus row in `remaining_papers_manual_check.md`, not the paper ID. The folder contains 23 PDF files, 21 unique readable PDFs after ignoring the two byte-identical duplicate copies of SurvITE and the two byte-identical duplicate copies of the Balzer adaptive-pair-matching paper. Duplicate corpus rows were not merged. Zone.Identifier files were ignored.

The downloaded PDFs were the primary evidence. Titles, authors, DOI or URL identifiers, and paper content were used to match files to checklist rows. Simulation-methods sections, tables, figure captions, appendices, supplementary material bundled with the PDFs, and source-version discrepancies were checked. The primary corpus value of J was coded, and other numerical repetition counts were recorded separately.

A count-specific precision, Monte Carlo-standard-error, power, pilot-variance, computational-budget, or other criterion is `explicit`. A recognizable convention or cited precedent without a formal count-specific calculation is `implicit_or_convention`, with criterion `precedent`. A reported outer count with no count-specific reason is `unjustified`, with criterion `none`. A non-unique corpus-to-paper mapping, incompatible source version, or unresolved outer-replication unit is `unclear_report`, with criterion `unclear`. `not_applicable` is reserved for a missing numeric J or unreadable full text. Generic DGP, sample-size, estimator, prior, matching, confidence-level, bootstrap, cross-validation, posterior-draw, MCMC, and optimization explanations were not counted unless explicitly tied to the outer replication count.

`sensitivity_to_J=yes` requires varying J itself or directly studying Monte Carlo error as a function of J. Varying only DGPs, sample sizes, estimators, priors, matching rules, hyperparameters, bootstrap draws, or other inner quantities is `no`. A mapping conflict is `unclear`; the no-numeric-J record is `not_applicable`.
