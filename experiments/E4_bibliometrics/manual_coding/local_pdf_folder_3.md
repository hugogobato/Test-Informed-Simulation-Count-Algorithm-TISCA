# J-justification classifications for `Remaining_Papers/Folder_3`

## Methods

The unit of analysis is the corpus row in `remaining_papers_manual_check.md`, not the paper ID. The 22 corpus rows represented by this folder were matched to 21 unique PDFs using title, authors, DOI, and paper content. The two Gao PDF copies have identical SHA-256 hashes and were treated as one source, while Gao corpus rows 34 and 93 remain separate records. Zone.Identifier files were ignored.

The downloaded PDFs were the primary evidence. The simulation-methods sections, relevant tables, figure captions, appendices, and locally available supplementary material were checked. The scanned Mosquera PDF was read from rendered pages. A count-specific precision, power, pilot-variance, or computational-budget rationale is `explicit`. A recognizable cited precedent or convention without a formal count-specific calculation is `implicit_or_convention`. A reported outer count without a count-specific rationale is `unjustified`. A count-to-source or outer-unit conflict is `unclear_report`. No Folder_3 record was `not_applicable`, because every record had a readable local PDF. Design choices, sample sizes, bootstrap draws, cross-validation folds, MCMC iterations, tree counts, and generic robustness statements were not treated as J justifications unless the paper connected them specifically to J.

`sensitivity_to_J=yes` would require varying J itself in a way that studies Monte Carlo error or sensitivity to J. Varying only DGPs, sample sizes, estimators, priors, matching rules, model hyperparameters, or inner fitting quantities is coded `no`. Mapping conflicts are coded `unclear`. The CSV contains the complete requested field set; the table below provides one auditable Markdown row for every corpus record.
